Junctions: the two ways English joins ideas

Coordinating conjunctions — the FANBOYS — join two ideas of equal weight. Subordinating conjunctions run one idea into another. Learn the rules, practise them, then take the thirty-question test.

How to use this with a class

Teach it, practise it, test it — in one lesson

Put the lesson on the board and work through the slides together. Then let students take the practice activities at their own pace; each one is short enough to finish in a few minutes and can be repeated as often as they like. Keep the test for the end of the lesson, or set it as homework — the best score is remembered on the device.

No internet needed. Everything in this page works offline once the file is open, so it runs from a memory stick, a school laptop or a tablet.
