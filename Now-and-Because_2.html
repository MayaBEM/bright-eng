Now & Because

Two grammar tools in one app. Say what is happening right now with the Present Continuous, then join your ideas together with conjunctions. Learn it, practise it, then test yourself — every answer is explained.

What this app covers

Present ContinuousConjunctions
Positive — am / is / are + verb-ing Coordinating — FANBOYS (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so)
Negative — am not / isn't / aren't Subordinating — because, since, although, while, if
Questions — Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing? Correlative — both…and, either…or, neither…nor, not only…but also
Spelling of -ing and verbs we do not use in the continuous Comma rules and subject–verb agreement

Lesson

Seven slides. Read them in order the first time — slides 1–4 are the Present Continuous, slides 5–7 are conjunctions.

Practice

Every answer is checked and explained straight away. Do the activities in any order, as many times as you like.

Test

30 questions · three parts

  • Part A — Present Continuous: form, negatives, questions, spelling (10)
  • Part B — Conjunctions: coordinating, subordinating, correlative (10)
  • Part C — Mixed: find the mistake and join the ideas (10)

You can answer in any order, skip a question and come back, and press Finish whenever you want. Every question is marked the moment you answer it, and you get the full explanations at the end.
