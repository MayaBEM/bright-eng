Grade 4 | Travel Vocabulary, in/at/on, Past Continuous + Past Simple (when), Phonics /eə/

Quick Review
- in for months/seasons/long periods (in March, in the summer, in the morning)
- at for clock times/specific points (at 3 o'clock, at night)
- on for days (on Friday, on Tuesdays)
- Past Continuous + Past Simple: an ongoing action (was/were + V-ing) interrupted by a short action (V2), joined by "when". Example: "I was walking up the wall, when I had a little fall."
